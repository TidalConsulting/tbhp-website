Frequently Asked

Questions Tampa Bay sellers ask us

How do you come up with your offer?

We look at recent comparable sales in your neighborhood, the current condition of your home, and what it will take to renovate it for the next family. We'll walk you through the numbers so nothing is a black box.

Are there any fees or commissions?

No. We don't charge commissions, listing fees, or closing-cost markups. The cash offer you accept is what you receive at closing.

How fast can you close?

As quickly as 7 days with a reputable Tampa-area title company. If you need more time, that's fine too — pick any date that works for you.

Do I need to make repairs or clean out the house?

Never. Leave what you want, take what you want — we handle the rest.

Do you buy tenant-occupied properties?

Yes. We regularly buy rentals with active leases or difficult tenants. You don't need to handle an eviction before selling.

Is there any obligation to accept your offer?

Zero. Our offers are free and no-obligation. If the number isn't right for you, there's no pressure — and no follow-up spam.
